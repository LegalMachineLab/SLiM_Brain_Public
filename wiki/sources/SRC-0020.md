## What the source argues

Zhu et al. (2025), on the evidence of the abstract alone (the file in the corpus holds only the paper's first page), report that dealmaking between LLM agents acting for consumers and merchants is an inherently imbalanced game, with large disparities between agents in the deals they obtain [CLM-0020-001], that LLMs' behavioural anomalies might cause financial loss such as overspending or unreasonable deals [CLM-0020-002], and conclude that users should be careful when delegating business decisions to LLM agents despite the efficiency automation brings [CLM-0020-003].
