## What the source argues

Hammond (2023), in a keynote abstract, holds that generative AI's impact differs from earlier technologies because it is grounded in language and therefore reaches language-grounded fields such as law [CLM-0042-001], that understanding it requires distinguishing skill in how to say things from knowledge of what to say [CLM-0042-002], and that systems synthesising data, analytics and language models can be both expressive and truthful [CLM-0042-003].

## Claims

### Descriptive

- Hammond (2023) state that The impact of generative AI systems such as ChatGPT differs from that of earlier technologies because it is grounded in language; as a result it is affecting fields that have been somewhat resistant to technological change in the past, and fields that are themselves grounded in language, such as law, are now trying to find ways to adapt to the technology.
